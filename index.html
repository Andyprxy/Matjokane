OUR STORY

A name held close.
A spirit carried forward.

Founded in 2008, Matjokane Funeral Services is a proudly 100% Black-owned funeral home in Nellmapius, Pretoria. Rooted in our community, we bring compassion, dignity and thoughtful care to every farewell.

We carry forward the vision of our beloved founder, Oupa Matjokane — honouring his legacy through the families we serve and the care we give.
