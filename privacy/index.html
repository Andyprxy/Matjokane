How this website presentation handles your enquiry.

A simple, direct conversation

This static website does not store enquiry details or submit them to a website database. The form creates a WhatsApp link containing the name, service interest and message you enter.

Continuing passes those details to WhatsApp for you to review. Sending the message shares it with the business. WhatsApp’s own privacy terms apply.

Sensitive information

Do not include identity numbers, banking details or medical records in the form. Ask the team how to share any necessary documents.

External services

Google Maps, Facebook and linked news coverage are external websites. This presentation uses no analytics or advertising scripts. External media added later may share connection information with its provider.

Before publication

This is a website concept. The business must approve its privacy notice, information contact and data handling procedures before public launch.
